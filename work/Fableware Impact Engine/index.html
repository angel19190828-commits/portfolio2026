01 Overview

Project Overview

Fableware’s Impact Engine transforms complex player choices into personalized summaries and shareable digital keepsakes. As the Lead Design Engineer, I focused on the player-facing experience, including the Player Summary flow, card system, front-end prototype, and developer handoff.

05 Internal Testing

Testing Comprehension and Flow

Internal testing focused on whether users understood the summary, connected the cards to their previous choices, and found the experience meaningful.

01
The connection between player choices and cards needed to be clearer.
02
Too much text made the result feel like a report instead of a keepsake.
03
Users wanted to select and explore individual cards.

Design Response

  • Clarified the relationship between choices and tags
  • Reduced unnecessary text
  • Strengthened card hierarchy
  • Added selectable card states
  • Separated View Deck and Share actions
